{ Hookdeck + Retell }

A lead arrives. Retell calls. Hookdeck reports back.

Start a call from the browser (or, optionally, from a phone number) and Retell's agent talks to the lead with the lead's details as dynamic variables. The call's call_started, call_ended and call_analyzed events come back through Hookdeck, which verifies x-retell-signature, deduplicates and retries, and update the lead below.

New lead

Talks to the agent from your microphone. No phone number needed.

Optional: call a phone instead. Needs a Retell phone number you bought (RETELL_FROM_NUMBER). There is no phone field: the call goes to TEST_LEAD_PHONE_NUMBER from .env.

Failure injection

Make this app misbehave, then watch Hookdeck absorb it: the event stays queued and is retried until the app answers 2xx. Retell itself is never kept waiting.
