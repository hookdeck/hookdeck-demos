How it works

Retell's agent, Sam, calls the lead with the lead's details as dynamic variables. In the browser you answer as the lead through your microphone, or optionally take the call on a phone. The call's call_started, call_ended and call_analyzed events come back through Hookdeck, which verifies x-retell-signature, deduplicates and retries, and update the leads below. Watch it in the Data flow pane.

About Sam

Sam opens with "Hi, is that Alex?" and then confirms it is the right person, asks whether now is a good time, confirms interest in the property, asks about budget and move date, and offers a viewing. Replies stay short, one question at a time. Sam never invents property details; anything unknown goes to a colleague.

Set up the browser call

Add RETELL_PUBLIC_KEY to .env: in the Retell dashboard open API Keys, then Public Keys, create a key, add "localhost" to its allowed domains, and copy the key (starts with public_key_). It is safe in the browser; it only works on the domains you allow. Then restart the app.

Call a phone instead

Needs a Retell phone number you bought (RETELL_FROM_NUMBER). There is no phone field: Sam calls TEST_LEAD_PHONE_NUMBER from .env.

Failure injection

Make this app misbehave, then watch Hookdeck absorb it: the event stays queued and is retried until the app answers 2xx. Retell itself is never kept waiting.

Retry ladder

When a call ends with dial_no_answer, dial_busy or voicemail_reached, the app schedules the next attempt 2 minutes later, up to three attempts. It does not dial by itself: when the countdown ends, the call button rings the lead again. After the last miss, the lead reads "Gave up".
